How to play

  • Move all 15 of your checkers into your home board (bottom right, or bottom left when the phone is upright), then bear them off. First to bear off all 15 wins.
  • Tap Roll. Each die is a separate move; doubles give four moves.
  • Tap one of your checkers, then a highlighted spot to move it there. You can tap a spot two dice away to use both dice on one checker.
  • You can't land on a point holding two or more opposing checkers. Landing on a single one (a blot) sends it to the bar; it must re-enter before anything else moves.
  • You must use both dice if you can (or the larger one if only one can be used).
  • When bearing off, double-tap a checker to take it straight off.
  • When your moves are used up the turn passes automatically after a moment. Tap Undo before then to take a move back.
  • Scoring: 1 point, 2 for a gammon (loser bore off none), 3 for a backgammon (loser also still has a checker on the bar or in the winner's home board).

Narde

  • All 15 checkers start on one point. Both stacks begin on your right-hand side and the players move towards each other, as in backgammon.
  • No hitting: you can’t land on any point with an opposing checker, even a single one.
  • Your first checker must travel all the way round into your home board before any other checker may move. If it can only use one of the dice, the other is skipped.
  • Until it has, doubles don’t count: roll again until you get a non-double.
  • Scoring: the winner scores the number of checkers they’re ahead by when they bear off their last one (15 minus the loser’s borne-off checkers). A Mars — the loser bore off none — is worth 15.
  • The first to 31 points wins the match.
